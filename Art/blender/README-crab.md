# Hero crab: how to rebuild everything

Everything is generated by headless Blender 5.2 LTS scripts (numpy geometry, no GUI, no external assets).
Run from the repo root. Cycles bakes use 4 CPU threads.

```
B=~/.local/bin/blender
$B -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python Art/blender/build_crab.py -- --stage all     # ~7 min
$B -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python Art/blender/rig_animate_crab.py              # ~20 s
$B -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python Art/blender/verify_crab_exports.py           # prints CRAB_VERIFY_OK
```

## Order and outputs

1. `build_crab.py --stage all` = four stages, each also runnable alone with `--stage <name>`:
   - `model`: procedural parts (`crab_lib.py`) become the ~25k triangle game mesh with a packed 2048 UV atlas,
     and a ~465k face high poly (subdivision plus displacement from `crab_detail.py`: carapace grooves, rim,
     granulation, claw tubercles, keels and finger teeth, leg joint grooves, ridges and spines).
     Writes `Art/blender/_work/crab_model.blend`.
   - `bake`: Cycles CPU bakes at 2048, one part at a time (each part is projected only onto its own high poly, so an arm
     passing through the shell cannot leave ghost patches on the carapace): tangent normal (DirectX, green down), ambient
     occlusion and displacement height, plus position, normal, part id and arc maps from the game mesh.
     About 6 min. Writes `_work/bake_maps.npz`.
   - `paint`: `crab_paint.py` hand-paints every texel from its 3D surface position (so colour is continuous over UV
     seams) and writes `Art/export/textures/T_FiddlerCrab_BaseColor.png`, `_Normal.png`, `_ORM.png`.
   - `lowblend`: game mesh plus `MI_FiddlerCrab` shader, saved as `_work/crab_lowpoly.blend`.
   Use `--stage paint` alone to iterate on colours (about 40 s); it reuses the baked maps.
2. `rig_animate_crab.py`: builds the 49 bone armature from the joint spec in `crab_lib.bone_specs()`, skins the mesh,
   bakes the four clips (`crab_motions.py`, `crab_anim.py`, `crab_skel.py`) and exports
   `Art/export/meshes/SK_FiddlerCrab.fbx` and `Art/export/anims/A_FiddlerCrab_{Idle,Scuttle,Dance,Dash}.fbx`.
   Also writes `_work/crab_rigged.blend` (all four actions) for previews.
3. `verify_crab_exports.py`: re-imports every FBX in Blender and checks bone names and hierarchy, one material
   `MI_FiddlerCrab`, triangle count, at most 4 influences, size, 30 fps, clip lengths, root never moves, loops close,
   feet never below Z = 0.
4. Previews (`render_crab_previews.py`, `<blend> <outdir> <mode> [engine] [extra]`), all into `Art/previews/`
   (Eevee works headless here, so no Cycles is needed):
   ```
   B=~/.local/bin/blender; R="--python Art/blender/render_crab_previews.py --"; BL=Art/blender/_work/crab_rigged.blend
   $B -b --factory-startup $R $BL Art/previews turn BLENDER_EEVEE        # crab_turnaround.png + turn_*.png
   $B -b --factory-startup $R $BL Art/previews gamecam BLENDER_EEVEE     # 55 deg down, 13 m, looking along +X
   CRAB_SHEET_VIEW=front3q $B -b --factory-startup $R $BL Art/previews sheet BLENDER_EEVEE Idle
   CRAB_SHEET_VIEW=rear3q  $B -b --factory-startup $R $BL Art/previews sheet BLENDER_EEVEE Scuttle
   CRAB_SHEET_VIEW=front   $B -b --factory-startup $R $BL Art/previews sheet BLENDER_EEVEE Dance
   CRAB_SHEET_VIEW=front3q $B -b --factory-startup $R $BL Art/previews sheet BLENDER_EEVEE Dash
   $B -b --factory-startup $R $BL Art/previews close BLENDER_EEVEE       # detail views
   ```
   `frames` renders single frames (`Dance:0,15,30`) and `sheet` also accepts custom frames (`"Idle:0,30,45"`).
   Engine `CYCLES` (CPU, 4 threads, denoised) is also supported.

`check_auto_weights.py` is a diagnostic only (see Skinning).

`Art/blender/_work/` holds intermediate .blend and .npz files (about 100 MB). It is safe to delete and should be
git-ignored.

## Contract facts, verified

- Units and orientation: Blender 1 unit = 1 m, the crab faces +X and its major claw is on Blender -Y. The FBX export uses
  Blender's default axes (`-Z` forward, `Y` up), no leaf bones, all bones exported. Orientation was checked in a
  throwaway UE 5.8 project (crab faces +X, `claw_major_1` at Y = +37 cm, 49 bones, `root` at the origin); that run used the
  older export below, so re-verify scale and orientation in the game project after your import.
- **Export scale fix (animation clips were 100x too small in Unreal).** The wrong setting was `apply_unit_scale=True`
  with `apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_NONE'` on a rig authored in metres: the exporter put `Lcl Scaling = 100` on the
  `Armature` node while every bone translation (static and animated) stayed in metres. Unreal applies the node scale to
  the reference pose of the skeletal mesh but not to animation tracks, so clips carried tracks in metres against a
  reference pose in centimetres and the skeleton collapsed to a point. Now `rig_animate_crab.py` exports from a
  centimetre copy of the rig (armature, mesh and keys multiplied by 100 in Blender only for export) with
  `apply_unit_scale=False` and `global_scale=0.01` (with unit scale off the exporter multiplies by 100, so the net is
  exactly 1). Result in the raw FBX files: file unit centimetres, no node has a scale, and the same bone reads the same in
  the mesh and every clip, for example `leg_R1_1` local translation (-39.5, 20.0, 5.0) in `SK_FiddlerCrab.fbx` and in the
  first key of all four clips, `body` (0, 30.7, -1.1) in Idle (before: (-0.395, 0.2, 0.05) and (0, 0.307, -0.011) with
  `Armature` scaled 100). `verify_crab_exports.py` now parses the raw FBX (not the importer) and fails if any node has a
  scale, if a clip track disagrees with the mesh reference pose, or if translations are not in centimetres.
- Origin on the ground between the feet; foot tips at Z = 0 in the rest pose. Carapace about 100 cm wide, 221 cm
  across with legs.
- Mesh: 25,108 triangles (high poly for baking: about 465k faces), one material slot `MI_FiddlerCrab`, at most 3 influences per vertex (limit is 4).
- Textures: 2048 px, `T_FiddlerCrab_BaseColor.png` (sRGB), `_Normal.png` (DirectX, green down, import as normal map,
  not sRGB), `_ORM.png` (linear, R = AO, G = roughness, B = metallic = 0).
- Clips: 30 fps, in place, `root` never moves, planted feet on Z = 0 (within 0.3 mm). Every clip has one key per frame
  including the last frame, so a loop of N frames has N+1 keys and the last key equals the first (Unreal reports N frames).
  - Idle 4.0 s (120 frames): breathing, eyestalk scanning and twitches, minor claw grooms the mouth twice, major claw
    sways and flexes, toe taps.
  - Scuttle 0.8 s (24 frames): travels toward the crab's right, metachronal gait (front to back wave, sides in
    opposition), stance 60 percent, swing arcs are time symmetric so reversed playback also works, body bob at twice
    the stride rate, claw carried up.
  - Dance 3.2 s (96 frames): six beat-synced claw waves at t = 0, 0.5 ... 2.5 s (120 bpm) with anticipation dip and
    overshoot, body lifted 10 cm on stretched legs, diagonal leg groups tap in alternation, body shimmy, eyestalk bobs,
    minor claw flourish on the off-beat, finger snap at the peak.
  - Dash 10 frames (0.333 s): crouch, then burst with legs at double speed, claw tucks, eyes fold back.

## Deviations from the contract

- Dash is 0.333 s, not 0.35 s: 0.35 s is 10.5 frames at 30 fps.
- Dance: 3.2 s at 0.5 s per beat is 6.4 beats. The clip has six on-beat waves and the last interval is stretched by
  0.2 s so the loop lands on beat one again. Loop is seamless, but the music grid drifts by 0.2 s per pass if a 120 bpm
  track is played over a looping clip.
- `claw_major_thumb` exists and is parented to `claw_major_3` but has no vertex weights (the fixed finger is skinned to
  the palm, as the contract says). It is never animated.

## Skinning

Blender's automatic weights were tried first (`check_auto_weights.py`): heat weighting takes under a second but 6,098 of
12,592 vertices ended up with more than 1 percent weight on another limb's bones (the carapace picked up leg and claw
bones, the major arm picked up body bones). The shipped weights fix that with rules per limb: the carapace is 100 percent
`body`, each leg and claw segment is rigid with a narrow blend across each joint, hips and shoulders blend into `body`,
and nothing crosses limbs. `verify_weights` in `rig_animate_crab.py` poses one limb at a time and asserts that no other
part moves (leg to leg, claw, eye and mouth checked).

## Known limits and things I would still improve

- No LODs, no collision, no morph targets.
- Shell detail is a 2048 normal map (about 6 px per cm); the granulation is subtle at game distance.
- The Unreal Skeleton asset is named `SK_FiddlerCrab_Skeleton` by the importer; rename or set it in `build_content.py`.
